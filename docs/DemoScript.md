# DemoScript.md

**Phase 6 of 8 · Challenge 1: Intelligent Conversational AI for the KSP Crime Database**

*Timed to the template's ~3-minute target (slide 13). Record with captions burned in — judges may watch on mute while reviewing many submissions back to back. Use real synthetic data throughout, not placeholder Lorem Ipsum; the credibility of the whole submission rests partly on the demo looking like it runs on an actual dataset, not a mockup.*

---

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook

**On screen:** Title card, then straight into the live app — no long intro animation.
**Narration:** *"Karnataka's 1,100+ police stations already generate digital crime data — but getting an answer from it still means a manual request and a multi-day wait. Setu turns that into a conversation."*

## 0:15–0:45 — English Query

**On screen:** Type or speak an English question into the chat, e.g., *"Show me break-ins reported in [district] in the last month using a similar entry method."*
**Narration (over the answer appearing):** *"Ask a question the way you'd ask a colleague. Setu retrieves the actual matching case records and answers with them cited — not a guess."*
**Show:** the answer, with visible source citations.

## 0:45–1:15 — Kannada Voice Query

**On screen:** Switch to voice, ask the same style of question in Kannada.
**Narration:** *"And it works the same way in Kannada, by voice — because most officers on the ground don't work in English first."*
**Show:** the Kannada transcription appearing, then the Kannada-language answer.

## 1:15–1:45 — Network Visualization

**On screen:** From an answer mentioning a named individual, click through to the network graph.
**Narration:** *"One click surfaces who's connected to whom across cases — links an investigator would otherwise have to piece together manually."*
**Show:** the interactive graph, expand one node.

## 1:45–2:15 — Proactive Hotspot Alert

**On screen:** Switch to the Alerts panel, already showing a surfaced pattern.
**Narration:** *"Setu doesn't just wait to be asked. It surfaces emerging patterns on its own — based on modus operandi, timing, and location. Never on who someone is."*
**Show:** a hotspot/cluster alert with its case-evidence explanation visible.

## 2:15–2:45 — Explainability & Export

**On screen:** Return to an earlier answer, show the reasoning trail, then export to PDF.
**Narration:** *"Every answer shows its work — and exports straight into a case file, with the audit trail intact."*
**Show:** the exported PDF opening briefly.

## 2:45–3:00 — Close

**On screen:** Brief architecture/services slide or Catalyst logo integration shot.
**Narration:** *"Built natively on Zoho Catalyst's own AI stack — bilingual, explainable, and ready to grow from one district to all 1,100 stations."*

---

## Production Notes

- Rehearse the full flow at least twice before recording — the Odisha AI Command Centre lesson (`CompetitorAnalysis.md` §5) is specifically that things which work once in a quiet test don't always work live; don't let the demo video be the first time this exact sequence runs start to finish.
- Have a fallback recording of each segment in case one live take glitches — faster to swap a 30-second clip than re-record the whole thing.
- Keep captions accurate to what's actually said, in both English and Kannada segments.
- **Grand Finale contingency** *(added Phase 7 review)*: the live 26 Sep demo depends on Zoho Catalyst itself being up, which is outside the team's control. Have a full pre-recorded run-through ready to show if the live system is unreachable on demo day — this isn't a failure of the pitch, it's what a team that's actually thought about production readiness does.
